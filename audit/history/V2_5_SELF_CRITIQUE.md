# v2.5 Self-Critique

## Verdict
v2.5 improves operational durability without adding many new mechanics. The biggest improvement is the freshness/snapshot layer, because it protects new sessions from stale context.

## Remaining risks
- `BRAIN_SNAPSHOT.md` only works if updated during night/weekly review.
- Recovery streaks may motivate or may become shame; test for one week only.
- System-note exit could become another preference-capture burden; skip on Red days.
- File count remains high, though loading priority is cleaner.

## Watch points
After one week, check:
- Was snapshot updated at least 4 times?
- Did new sessions start faster?
- Did the PM stage field become more accurate?
- Did the command menu feel easier?
- Did system-note exit produce useful preference signals or noise?

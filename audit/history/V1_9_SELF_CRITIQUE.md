# v1.9 Self-Critique

## Verdict
v1.9 improves usability and closes high-risk practical gaps from v1.8. It is more likely to be used on Red/Yellow days because short review paths are now default.

## Strongest improvements
- Night review friction reduced.
- Timesheet anchor made explicit.
- PM fake progress/stage stall handled.
- Shame thoughts standardized without over-processing.
- No-check-in recovery mode makes the assistant less dependent on perfect user initiation.

## Remaining watch points
- Too many core files can still overwhelm weaker model implementations. Use file-loading priority.
- If the shame sentence bank feels repetitive, it should be revised after real use, not expanded preemptively.
- If the 5-minute weekly review is still skipped, reduce it further to stability score + one target.
- If PM track creates pressure during job-risk weeks, put PM into maintenance mode rather than removing it.

## One-week test
Use v1.9 for 7 days. Track:
- Did night review happen at least 4 nights?
- Did timesheet/status capture happen at least 4 workdays?
- Was PM/career output produced at least twice?
- Did short mode reduce avoidance of the system?
